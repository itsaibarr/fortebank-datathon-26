# Legal and visual review

Use [legal-reasoning.md](legal-reasoning.md) for official BM25/exact retrieval and
condition adjudication; [person-tracking.md](person-tracking.md) for private human
review. `forte annotations-check` validates source references and independent
review scopes; it never approves semantic truth. Draft rule cards stay draft,
unknown recording dates stay unknown and reserved/jury data never enters selection.
No training or weight updates are part of the product. Visual, geometry, identity,
role and legal reviews remain separate. Corrections persist immutably; later model
consumption must be explicitly implemented and verified.
