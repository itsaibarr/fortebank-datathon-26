## Full-product comparison

### 1. Acquisition, archive and timestamp handling

Axxon documents device discovery/manual/CSV onboarding, supported-device states and camera identity independent of display names. Current external-archive documentation exposes time references and gaps/overlaps; historical Offline Analytics describes importing/indexing recordings. Clock synchronization and matching client/server builds are operational requirements. These mechanisms justify stable source IDs and explicit coverage, without proving our latency or arbitrary-file compatibility. [Axxon device onboarding](https://docs.axxonsoft.com/confluence/spaces/ONE2025/pages/314536014/Search%2Badd%2Bconfigure%2Band%2Bremove%2BIP%2Bdevices), [external archives](https://docs.axxonsoft.com/confluence/spaces/ONE2025/pages/314538692/Viewing%2Bexternal%2Barchive), [requirements](https://docs.axxonsoft.com/confluence/spaces/ONE2025/pages/314535866/Axxon%2BOne%2Brequirements).

Mikshi markets existing IP-camera/NVR compatibility; its main pipeline concerns live feeds. Uploaded-video analysis, retention, stream recovery, ingest-clock alignment and original-PTS guarantees were not established. ForteBank keeps its working upload pipeline and source timebase. Live-camera integration is deferred until actual interfaces and deployment ownership justify it. Findings AX-002–003, AX-030 and MK-004–005 carry these boundaries.

### 2. Scene analysis, tracking and adaptation

Axxon neural tracking exposes sampling rate, trigger thresholds, track retention, object classes and zones. Multi-camera continuity additionally needs map calibration, camera relationships and similarity modules. Tracking therefore has configuration and resource dependencies; it supplies neither bank-role truth nor an identity accuracy result. [Neural tracker](https://docs.axxonsoft.com/confluence/spaces/ONE2025/pages/314536630/Configuring%2Bthe%2BNeural%2Btracker), [multi-camera tracker](https://docs.axxonsoft.com/confluence/spaces/ONE2025/pages/398917757/Multi-camera%2Btracker).

Mikshi describes presence, interaction and restricted-zone modules, but its public material does not establish tracker identity continuity, checkpoints, training procedure or measured action reasoning. Configuration is not training. Our detector geometry, tracker identity, human role fact, view relationship and legal conclusion retain separate origins. This product slice does not modify CORE_W2's tracker, model or legal contracts; CORE_W2's separately integrated repairs have their own acceptance record. Findings AX-004–005 and MK-009, MK-025 support this separation.

### 3. Search and investigation

Axxon archive search scopes camera/time, event type, initiator and assessment comments. Results return to contextual archive playback. Its VLM marketing describes text/frame-vector similarity; Meta-detector documentation describes consecutive matching measurements, not demonstrated causal action understanding. [Event search](https://docs.axxonsoft.com/confluence/spaces/ONE2025/pages/314538772/Events%2Bsearch), [result navigation](https://docs.axxonsoft.com/confluence/spaces/ONE2025/pages/314538879/Working%2Bwith%2Bsearch%2Bresults), [VLM description](https://www.axxonsoft.com/ai-video-analytics/vision-language-model-vlm).

Mikshi's [person-search page](https://mikshi.mantratec.com/usecase/person-vehicle-search) expressly distinguishes appearance-attribute image matching from continuous tracking and biometric identity. It claims timestamp/location results and reports; ranking and real query behavior were not exercised. AD-01 adopts scoped discovery using existing local text and IDs. It adds no embedding or semantic claim. Axxon's [Meta search](https://docs.axxonsoft.com/confluence/spaces/ONE2025/pages/393412610/Meta%2Bsearch) documents grouping matches separated by at most five seconds. That rule is rejected for actor-sensitive incidents: nearby events may involve different people or operations. Every original event ID and adjacency remains intact.

### 6. Operational analytics and rule configuration

Axxon queue detection needs scene zones, size/sensitivity and count thresholds; area occupancy measures occupied area fraction, and loitering expresses a temporal predicate. These differ from waiting time, staff availability and legal duties. [Queue configuration](https://docs.axxonsoft.com/confluence/spaces/ONE2025/pages/314537282/Configuring%2Bthe%2BQueue%2Bdetector), [occupancy](https://docs.axxonsoft.com/confluence/spaces/ONE2025/pages/349307124/Functions%2Bof%2Bthe%2BArea%2Boccupancy%2Bdetector).

Mikshi describes configurable counter/time queue thresholds, unattended-duration coverage, required staff windows and anonymous aggregate dwell patterns. Its [restricted-area](https://mikshi.mantratec.com/usecase/restricted-area-access-monitoring) material treats visual monitoring as complementing access control. Camera presence alone establishes no authorization or attendance fact. Zones, queues, dwell and occupancy remain later hardening because reviewed geometry, role inputs, coverage and calibration are missing. We preserve visible unexamined/unclear intervals now instead of displaying unsupported operational metrics. Findings AX-019–022 and MK-007–013 record individual mechanisms and limitations.
