# ForteBank video review

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Bank control and audit staff reviewing cash-operation recordings, as described in
the supplied implementation and jury brief. The exact production access policy is open.

## Product Purpose

Connect a participant, observed action, original video interval and evidence to the
conditions of an official Kazakhstan provision. Prepare a checkable review, retaining
uncertainty and human corrections. Recognition and legal accuracy are not established.

## Operating Context

Upload a MOV, wait for bounded local analysis, seek an event, inspect source frames and
provision conditions, then record a scoped correction. Development footage stays in the
authorized private environment. No remote AI, face identification or audio analysis.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Preserve the v1 jury API and the explicit 8B rollback. The versioned reasoning API exposes
supported violation, no violation observed within scope, insufficient evidence, and not
applicable separately from execution status. All inference weights are frozen. Draft
legal interpretations and sparse coverage cannot establish a validated violation or absence.
No reserved or jury data enters configuration selection. Session grouping remains unresolved.

## Brand Commitments

User selected Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind, shadcn preset `b3d6mT4h6m`, and Lucide icons.
ForteBank Datathon 26 is the project name; no commercial product name or bank logo is implied.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing source-PTS decoding and authenticated job mechanics; three official-law snapshots;
private provisional tracking and human correction revisions; limited earlier GPU runs that
failed recognition acceptance. Build artifacts contain no private recordings or examples.

## Product Principles

- Original source evidence remains recoverable.
- Visible observations, legal interpretation and human review remain distinct.
- Unknown facts and incomplete work remain visible.
- Corrections append provenance rather than replacing the model's original proposal.
